{%- comment -%} The course decision surface on /schulungen/: one card per course, replacing the four former w3schools-style colour buttons (IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md item 4.3/4.4). Each card answers what a buyer needs before clicking deeper: what the course is, who it is for, who teaches it, how long it takes, when it next runs, and which certificate it leads to. Dates, duration, trainers, certification and credits come from site.data.trainings (synced, never hand-edited — see CLAUDE.md); the German one-liners live here because the data's `blurb` fields are English. Date filtering matches course-bridge.html: skip cancelled/waitlist/full and past dates, take the earliest remaining START. A course without an open date renders without the date row instead of dying. MSA is special-cased (2026-08-13, owner feedback): the standard offering is the 4-day German public course with two trainers; the 3-day English online run (other trainer) is the exception. The flagship card therefore leads with the next PUBLIC date and lists the next ONLINE date as a labelled alternative, rather than letting an earlier online date pose as "the" next MSA. Trainers: per-date `trainers` overrides the course-level list. No price anywhere — `pricing` is German prose, mostly absent (see the Blocked item in IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md). Links are written out as /info-/. Never emit course.url or date.url: both are absolute https://www.arc42.de/... addresses, and `make test-theme` fails the build if that host appears anywhere in _site.
