{%- comment -%} iSAQB credit points for one course, in the page language. Params: credits (a courses[].credit_points object), lang ("de"|"en") de: 20 methodische und 10 technische en: 20 methodology and 10 technical The noun is deliberately NOT part of the value. Every call site already labels the row ("iSAQB-Punkte" on a card, "iSAQB credits" in the booking summary), and repeating it produced "iSAQB credits: 20 ... credits". This replaced a hand-written German string on the course, which appeared untranslated on every English page. Counts are integers so they stay countable; the three keys are iSAQB's own categories (Methodik, Technik, Kommunikation).
