Every type without a `when` below is rendered by the data-driven standard card timeline_card.html from course and course_date alone, so a new course needs no template. A hand-made timeline_.html, with its `when`, overrides the standard card for its course while it exists. Parameters: - type: Course type. Only the standard card reads them; "course_date" because "date" is the label. - position: "left" or "right" for timeline positioning - date: Course date label - location: Course location (not needed for online courses) - anchor_id: Unique anchor for linking - sold_out: true/false (optional) - status_note: why the card is sold out, in the page language (waitlist vs full) - hide_register: true hides the Anmeldung button (full only; waitlist stays registrable) - few_seats: Text to display for limited availability (optional) - pricing: Price sentence in the page language (optional) - trainer: Trainer names (optional) - credits: iSAQB credit points in the page language, without the noun (optional) - delivery_lang: language the course is HELD in ("de"|"en"); drives only the small note from timeline-language-note.html, never the card copy - page_lang: presentation language of the page (default "de"); only the standard card reads it, the hand-made cards are German throughout {% endcomment
